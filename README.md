# VetCare Platform

Centralized web platform for veterinary clinics: client and patient records, appointments, hospitalization and pharmacy inventory with role-based access.

## Modules

1. **Authentication & Access Control (RBAC):** registration, login, password recovery and permissions per role (Veterinarian, Receptionist, Administrator, Veterinary Assistant, Owner).
2. **Patient & Client Management:** owner and pet records, species-specific clinical records, vaccinations and prescriptions.
3. **Space & Appointment Management:** online booking, room and doctor scheduling, hospitalization map and reminders.
4. **Pharmacy & Inventory Management:** stock by batch, automatic deduction, low-stock and expiration alerts, and suppliers.
